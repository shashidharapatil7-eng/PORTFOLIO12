Getting started

Recommended order for filling in the site.

  1. Write your bio in About and add a profile photo in Settings.
  2. Add real work in Projects — mark 1–3 as Featured.
  3. Add credentials in Certifications as you earn them.
  4. Upload your latest PDF in Resume.
  5. Fill in Experience, Education, and social links / email in Settings.

Every save here appears on the live site within a second or two — nothing to download or redeploy.
